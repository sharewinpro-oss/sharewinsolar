Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about solar, subsidies, and installation.

What is the PM Surya Ghar subsidy amount? ▼

Under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, eligible residential consumers receive ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW+ systems. Subsidy is deposited via Direct Benefit Transfer after DISCOM commissioning.

Am I eligible for the subsidy? ▼

Residential consumers with valid identity proof, address proof, electricity connection, and roof/terrace access are typically eligible. State-specific requirements may apply.

How long does installation take? ▼

From application to DISCOM approval usually takes 30–60 days. Actual installation takes 3–5 days depending on system size.

What if my roof has shade? ▼

Our engineers assess shade patterns and design layouts optimizing sun exposure. Shaded portions are avoided, and microinverters can mitigate partial shading.

How much can I save annually? ▼

Savings depend on current EB bill, system size, and location. A typical 2 kW system saves ₹24,000–₹36,000 annually in Tamil Nadu.

Is maintenance required? ▼

Solar panels require minimal maintenance—occasional cleaning and annual DISCOM inspections. Inverters may need servicing after 5–10 years.

What warranty do you offer? ▼

We provide 25-year panel warranty, 10-year inverter warranty, and 5-year installation warranty covering workmanship and materials.

Can I sell excess power? ▼

With a net meter, excess power is fed to the grid and credited on your bill. You don't earn cash but get reduced electricity costs.

What happens during power cuts? ▼

Standard grid-tied systems work when the grid is active. Optional battery storage systems can provide backup during outages.

How is the system interconnected to the grid? ▼

Your solar system connects to a bidirectional net meter which measures power both to and from the grid, offsetting your consumption.

What happens after DISCOM approval? ▼

After approval and net meter commissioning, your system is live. Subsidy processing typically takes 2–4 weeks via DBT to your bank account.

Can I increase my system size later? ▼

Yes. Many customers expand systems 3–5 years later. We handle all documentation and DISCOM updates for seamless upgrades.
